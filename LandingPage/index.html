// HOW IT WORKS

From camera
to coaching

FitMentor processes camera frames, identifies body landmarks, and uses exercise-specific rules to track repetitions and surface workout feedback.

01

Capture

Your browser camera provides frames for the live workout session.

02

Detect pose

MediaPipe Pose estimates body landmarks from each processed frame.

03

Track movement

Exercise-specific rules interpret landmark positions and track repetitions.

04

Get feedback

The app presents workout guidance based on the detected exercise state.
